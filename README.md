## 💡 Understanding Test Results

The scanner provides data on how your connection passes through network filters. Here are the key indicators to watch:

- **DPI Detected:** This means your internet provider sees the content of your traffic.
- **VPN Obscured:** This indicates that your VPN tunnel stays hidden from external monitors.
- **Protocol Analysis:** This shows the specific rules networks use to identify your data packets. 
- **Latency:** This measures the time it takes for data to reach the target IP.

If the results show a "High Risk" warning, your traffic appears visible to your network operator. You may need to change your VPN protocols or adjust your routing settings.
